About Us

Why TactiGrid exists

TactiGrid was built to link coaches together and create a real community around session design. When I entered the sector, I found it surprisingly difficult to find the right coaching network — the niche people, the right contacts, and a place to ask questions without feeling lost.

I studied a degree in the game and wanted to reach out to coaches, learn from their experiences, and build my journey properly — but there wasn’t a clear space to do that. Now it’s here: a place to design, share, learn, and improve together.

What we’re building

A living library of drills and session ideas — powered by coach portfolios and real feedback from the network. The aim is simple: make planning easier, raise session quality, and help coaches improve faster.

  • Build drills visually in the designer
  • Share them to the community for feedback
  • Publish your best work into your portfolio
