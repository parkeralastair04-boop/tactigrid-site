About TactiGrid

Built by a Coach, for Coaches

TactiGrid was created to solve a common problem: finding a place where coaches can design sessions, share ideas and connect with others who are genuinely passionate about development.

After studying the game and recognising the importance of tactical and performance analysis, the vision became clear — create a platform that helps coaches learn together and improve faster.

Our Mission

To build the go-to platform for football coaches to design, collaborate, receive feedback and store their best session ideas in one place.
